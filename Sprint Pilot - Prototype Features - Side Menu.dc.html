Coolant Data Capture — Prototype Features

Sprint / TRUQ2 pilot · TMMAL · October 2026 · updated with Jeff Davis’s round-two feedback · side-menu version

What the app is

Coolant Data Capture replaces the paper and Excel coolant round. Fluid techs carry a tablet and record each check at the machine. The app works out concentration and dosing on the spot, flags anything out of range, alerts Group Leads when a collector fails, and builds the reports managers send to Toyota.

The pilot covers Sprint / TRUQ2: 132 systems across Shaft 1/2, 3/4 and 5/6, Carrier, Cap, Case, Manual Case and Assembly, plus 18 parts washers, taken from the Sprint master template. TNGA, V6T and 24T will be adapted from the pilot after plant walks.

A typical day

  1. The manager has assigned techs to each job for the week.
  2. The tech opens the Dashboard to see what's due, then works through the Daily Round.
  3. They do their other checks: bug slides, pH, washers, dust and mist collectors.
  4. A failed collector sends a text to both Group Leads for that area.
  5. At the end of the week the manager imports the General Stores and Leadec files, reviews the reports and sends them to Toyota.

Getting around: the side menu

This version moves navigation into a menu down the left edge. Every screen and rule is the same as the top-menu version; only the layout changes, so all the screenshots below show the side menu.

  • Top of the menu: the Chemico logo, the app name and the plant area switch (Sprint, TNGA, V6T, 24T).
  • Screens: one list from Dashboard to Master Data. The current screen is highlighted in cyan. Techs only see the screens their role allows.
  • Bottom of the menu: the Toyota logo with Toyota · TMMAL, and the Collapse menu button.
  • Top bar: just the date and week, Online/Offline status, Feedback and the demo role picker, so more of the screen is left for the tables.

1. Dashboard

  • Four counts: systems due today, completed, out of range, and missing comments.
  • Latest reading per system, filtered by line, shift and schedule group, 40 systems to a page.
  • Today's warnings list each missing reading or uncommented problem, with a button that goes straight to it.

2. Daily Round

  • The tech enters the refractometer reading (Brix). The app converts it to concentration % using the product's factor.
  • Below the low limit (critical) the row turns red and the app works out the litres (and gallons) needed to reach target. Above the high limit (non-critical) it turns yellow. The same red and yellow are used on every screen and report.
  • Tank level, skimmer, virgin or recycled fluid and the final reading are recorded on the same row.
  • Comments are picked from Jeff’s standard list or typed. Items ending in “-” get the date added, for example “+H2O-10/15”. A row still out of range after dosing can't be saved without a comment.
  • MDS-570 systems aren't refracted, so they're listed under the round heading and left off it.
  • Progress for the shift shows at the top. Each saved entry records who made it and when.

3. Assignments · manager

  • Separate lists for concentration rounds, bug slides and pH, dust and mist collectors, and washers.
  • Assign one system at a time, or a whole line or shift in one step.
  • Managers can move any system between Shift 1 and Shift 2. The round, dashboard and reports follow straight away.
  • Each tech has a color that carries through to the Daily Round and the check sheets, so everyone can see their own work.

4. Bug & pH

  • Slides are dipped Monday and read Wednesday. Groups A and B alternate by week.
  • Bacteria and fungus readings, action taken, and pH before and after.
  • Grotan, LA-33 and WR-90 doses are calculated with Jeff’s multipliers: sump L × 0.0015, × 0.0002 and × 0.001. Tap to record the dose added.
  • High counts or pH outside range are flagged with a short instruction, in red or yellow.

5. Washers

  • The tech enters the number of drops. Initial RI % = drops × the product’s drop factor.
  • Below range, the app works out the dose with Jeff’s formula: (target − RI) × 0.01 × sump L. The tech re-titrates and enters the Final RI.
  • pH, free oil, tank level, skimmer, WR-90 and a comment from the standard list are recorded on the same row.

6. Mix Tank

Raw fluid, water and mix sent to line, in litres. The app shows the expected strength from the mix ratio beside each reading and highlights any that don't match.

7. Dust, Mist & Skimmer checks

  • One tap per check: O for OK, X for Not Good. The tech’s initials are added.
  • Missed days show amber. Dust and mist collectors are kept separate, each with its own report.

Text alert to Group Leads

Shaft collectors go to the Shaft Group Leads (Melisha Martin, Moises Rodriguez); Carrier, Case and Cap go to the Carrier/Diff Group Leads (Jason Todd, Josh Reyer). Green and Yellow crews rotate every two weeks on Jeff’s dates: Yellow is on days now and Green moves to days on 11/2. Only Group Leads get the text. In the prototype the text is a preview only.

8. Leadec Log

The manager ticks a week or imports Leadec’s completion sheet. Rescheduled cleanings show amber with the reason, for example no G-2200T onsite. Cleanings also appear as markers on the trend charts.

9. Imports · manager

  • General Stores fluid orders: reads the all-area order calendar and compares Sprint/TRUQ2 orders with what techs recorded adding.
  • Leadec schedule: completed cleanings are ticked in the Leadec Log and rescheduled ones are flagged. Typos in machine numbers are matched, and machines not in the system list are called out.
  • Columns are matched by their headings, so the files don't need a fixed layout.

10. Reports

  • Ten reports: Weekly Status, Washer Report, Assembly Washer, Chemical Use, Bug and pH, Leadec Weekly, Dust Collectors, Mist Collectors, Skimmers and Trend Charts.
  • Each report has PDF and Excel export and a Send to distribution list button with Toyota’s four lists: Sprint/TRUQ2, TNGA, 103E/V6T and 152E/2.4T.
  • Internal techs see the same reports and exports, without the Send button.

11. Master Data · manager

The lists everything else depends on: systems and their limits, products, comment pick lists, report distribution lists, alert contacts, the holiday calendar and users. Changes apply straight away.

Also built in

  • Offline mode: techs keep working without Wi-Fi. Entries wait on the tablet and sync when it reconnects.
  • Feedback: reviewers can leave a note on any screen.
  • Plant areas: TNGA, V6T and 24T tabs are in place for the next phases.

About this prototype

  • It shows every screen and rule working, but has no database. Data resets when the page is reloaded.
  • Tech names, Group Leads, phone numbers, report lists and the order and Leadec files are Jeff Davis’s real data. Readings and history are still made up.
  • Still to confirm: the drop-to-RI factors for G-2200TU (0.1% per drop) and G-5300ME (0.15% per drop). Both are placeholders and can be changed in Master Data → Products.
  • Texts, emails and exports show a preview or confirmation only. Nothing is sent.
