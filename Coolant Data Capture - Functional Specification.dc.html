1. Purpose and scope

Coolant Data Capture (CDC) replaces the paper and Excel coolant round at TMMAL. Fluid techs record each check at the machine on a tablet. The app works out concentration and dosing, flags anything out of range, texts Group Leads when a collector fails, and builds the reports managers send to Toyota. This document states what Release 1 must do. Where a rule was confirmed by Jeff Davis it is stated as a requirement; anything not yet confirmed is listed in section 10.

In scope for Release 1

  • Sprint / TRUQ2: 132 systems across Shaft 1/2, 3/4 and 5/6, Carrier, Cap, Case, Manual Case and Assembly, plus 18 parts washers.
  • Every screen in prototype v3: Dashboard, Daily Round, Assignments, Bug & pH, Washers, Mix Tank, Dust, Mist & Skimmer, Leadec Log, Imports, Reports, Master Data, Users & Access.
  • Dust collector Forms export upload for all five Forms links: Sprint/TRUQ2, TNGA, V6T, 2.4T and Tool Regrind.
  • Text alerts to Group Leads, report email to Toyota distribution lists, the Toyota dust/mist file, and Power BI publishing.

Out of scope for Release 1

  • Coolant rounds for TNGA, V6T and 2.4T. They are adapted from the pilot after plant walks. The site switch and per-site access are built now so they can be added without rework.
  • In-line concentration and pH sensors (shown as Future in the prototype).
  • The prototype’s reviewer feedback button.

3. Functional requirements

Each requirement has an ID for test cases and change control. The prototype screen of the same name shows the intended behaviour.

3.1 Sign-in and navigation

SI-1Sign-in is two steps: choose the site, then the person. Only people with access to that site are listed, with their role there.
SI-2After sign-in, a welcome banner on the Dashboard shows site, role, shift and today’s assigned systems, with a Start my round button.
SI-3Sign-out is blocked while entries are waiting to sync. After sign-out the tablet returns to the sign-in screen.
SI-4Production sign-in uses the method agreed in open item T2. Contractor access stops automatically on its end date.
NV-1Navigation is a left side menu that collapses to icons. It lists only the screens the user’s role allows on the current site.
NV-2The site switch shows only the sites the user has access to.
NV-3On a phone the menu folds behind a menu button and the Daily Round shows one compact card per machine; tapping a card opens its details. One Save button saves every changed row.

3.2 Dashboard

DB-1Four counts for today: systems due, completed, out of range, and missing comments.
DB-2Latest reading per system, filterable by line, shift and schedule group, 40 systems to a page.
DB-3Today’s warnings list each missing reading or uncommented problem, with a link straight to the row.

3.3 Daily Round

DR-1Lists the systems due today (BR-7) for the chosen shift, grouped by line, with the assigned tech’s color on each row and shift progress at the top.
DR-2The tech enters the refractometer reading (Brix); the app shows concentration % (BR-1).
DR-3Below range the row turns red and shows litres and gallons to add (BR-2). Above range it turns yellow: non-critical, no product added. These colors are used on every screen and report.
DR-4Each row also records tank level, skimmer, product added, virgin or recycled, and the final reading.
DR-5Comments are picked from the standard list or typed. A row still out of range after dosing can’t be saved without a comment (BR-3).
DR-6Systems on a product with no refractive factor (MDS-570) are listed under the round heading and left off the round.
DR-7Rows the user can’t edit show a lock with the reason (other shift, entered by someone else, or older than 5 days).

3.4 Assignments

AS-1Separate assignment lists for concentration rounds, bug slides and pH, dust and mist collectors, and washers.
AS-2Assign one system, or a whole line or shift in one step.
AS-3Move any system between Shift 1 and Shift 2. The round, dashboard and reports follow straight away.
AS-4Each tech has a color that carries through to the Daily Round and check sheets.

3.5 Bug & pH

BP-1Every system shows each week. Systems not in this week’s slide group show NT for bacteria and fungus; pH is still checked (BR-9).
BP-2Records bacteria, fungus, action taken, and pH before and after.
BP-3Grotan, LA-33 and WR-90 doses are calculated from sump size (BR-5). Tap to record the dose added. A treated system has its Grotan dose filled in; a pH below range fills in the LA-33 dose.
BP-4Counts at limit or pH out of range are flagged with a short instruction, red for critical and yellow for high pH (BR-4, BR-6).

3.6 Washers

WA-1Weekly titration for all 18 Sprint washers. Inhibitor 42 (I-42) replaces G-2200TU on every washer.
WA-2The titration chart for each product in use is shown above the table, read from Master Data.
WA-3The tech enters the number of drops; Initial RI % is read from the chart (BR-10). The row notes when a between-rows count used the row below, and flags counts above the chart.
WA-4Below range the row turns red with the dose to add (BR-11). The tech re-titrates and enters the Final RI.
WA-5pH, free oil, tank level, skimmer, WR-90 and a comment are recorded on the same row. Low pH and heavy free oil are flagged; heavy oil with a broken skimmer prompts a work order note.

3.7 Mix Tank

MT-1Records today’s batch in litres: raw fluid, water, mix sent to line, and two Brix readings.
MT-2Shows each reading’s concentration beside the strength expected from the mix ratio and highlights any that don’t match (BR-8). The week’s log is shown below.

3.8 Dust, Mist & Skimmer checks

DM-1For now, dust collector checks come from TMMAL’s Microsoft Forms. The screen opens on a Forms upload tab.
DM-2The tab lists each Forms link (Sprint/TRUQ2, TNGA, V6T, 2.4T, Tool Regrind) with its line dropdown, last upload, next due date and status: Up to date, Due today, Overdue or Not uploaded.
DM-3Upload frequency is Weekly or Monthly, set by the manager. It starts Weekly and changes every due date when switched. The Plant Admin gets a reminder when an area is due.
DM-4The upload reads the Forms Excel export as downloaded (section 7.3). The area comes from the file name or the Department column. Uploading marks that area as uploaded today.
DM-5After upload, CDC shows for the chosen month: checks submitted, Not Good count, workdays with no check, a day-by-day sheet per line with the initials of who filled in the form, and every Not Good with equipment, details and actions taken, and reporter.
DM-6Open report opens the Dust Collectors report; Download for Toyota gives the PASS / FAIL file (section 7.4).
DM-7Sprint’s line dropdown uses its own product lines. Tool Regrind has one choice, and its export doesn’t need a Line column.
DM-8In-app check sheets stay available for Dust, Mist and Skimmer: a monthly sheet of workdays by area, one tap cycling blank → O → X, with the tech’s initials. Missed days show amber. Dust and mist collectors are kept separate, each with its own report.
DM-9The Toyota transfer lists every check for the month, with failures first, showing date, line, who checked it and whether the Group Leads were texted. It records when it was sent and prompts again when new checks arrive.

3.9 Text alerts

AL-1Marking a dust or mist collector X in the app texts both Group Leads for that area, and nobody else. The tech sees a message preview before it sends.
AL-2Shaft collectors go to the Shaft Group Leads; Carrier, Case and Cap go to the Carrier/Diff Group Leads. Where an area has one lead, that person covers both crews.
AL-3Green and Yellow crews rotate every two weeks (BR-12); each lead’s crew and shift show on the alert.
AL-4Every text sent is logged with time, recipient and collector.

3.10 Leadec Log

LD-1Every machine in Leadec’s scope, with a box for each week of the month. Ticking a week records the clean and recharge with initials and date. Future weeks are locked.
LD-2Rescheduled cleanings show amber with the reason.
LD-3Machines on Leadec’s sheet that aren’t in the coolant rounds are kept as Leadec-only for the cleaning record. They don’t need a line and never affect the rounds.
LD-4Cleanings show as markers on the trend charts.

3.11 Imports

IM-1Three Excel imports: General Stores fluid orders, Leadec schedule and completions, and the dust collector Forms export (section 7). Columns are matched by heading, so files don’t need a fixed layout.
IM-2Each import shows a preview of the rows found before it is applied, and can be cancelled.
IM-3General Stores: reads the all-area order calendar and compares Sprint/TRUQ2 orders with what techs recorded adding this month. Other areas are kept for their rollout.
IM-4Leadec: uploaded as it comes. Machine numbers are matched to the CDC system list, including known typos. Completed cleanings are ticked in the Leadec Log and rescheduled ones flagged.

3.12 Reports

RP-1Ten reports: Weekly Status, Washer Report, Assembly Washer, Chemical Use, Bug and pH, Leadec Weekly, Dust Collectors, Mist Collectors, Skimmers and Trend Charts.
RP-2Weekly Status: one page per line with daily concentration, virgin and recycled gallons added, bacteria, fungus, action, pH 8.9–9.6 and comments, and a checklist of open items to fix before sending.
RP-3Dust Collectors: from the in-app sheet or the uploaded Forms export, a line-by-line summary for the month (checked, OK, Not Good, no check, Not Good days, last check) and the Not Good details with actions taken.
RP-4Trend Charts: 11 weeks of concentration per system against its target range, with Leadec cleans marked.
RP-5Every report exports to PDF and Excel. Managers can send any report to one or more of Toyota’s four distribution lists: Sprint/TRUQ2, TNGA, 103E/V6T and 152E/2.4T. Sending is manual, never automatic.
RP-6Reports also publish to Power BI (open item T6).

3.13 Master Data

Site Managers and Plant Admins can change all master data themselves. Nothing is fixed in setup, and changes apply straight away to the round, dashboard and reports.

MD-1Systems: edit OP #, machine #, product, range and target in place; move a system between MWTH and TF or between shifts. Summary counts by schedule group and shift.
MD-2Products: add products, set or clear the refractive factor (blank = not refracted), with a worked example and the number of systems using each.
MD-3Titration charts: edit any drops → RI % row, add or delete rows, and start a chart for a new product.
MD-4Pick Lists: standard comments and pit water additions; edit, add or remove. Items ending in “-” get the date added when picked.
MD-5Distribution List: the four Toyota lists with names and emails.
MD-6Alert Contacts: Group Leads per area and crew, with phone numbers; each can be switched on or off.
MD-7Holiday Calendar: add and remove plant holidays (BR-7).

3.14 Users & Access

UA-1One role per person per site, or no access. The site switch and sign-in list follow straight away.
UA-2An optional end date per person; access stops on that date.
UA-3The permission matrix in section 2 is editable by a Plant Admin.
UA-4A Plant Admin can’t remove their own admin access, so a site is never left without one.

3.15 Offline working and audit

AU-1Techs keep working without Wi-Fi. Entries wait on the device and sync when it reconnects; the header shows Online/Offline and how many entries are waiting.
AU-2Every saved entry records who made it and when. Every edit records who changed what, from and to; the history opens from the row.
AU-3Deleting always asks for confirmation and is logged.

4. Business rules and calculations

IDRuleDefinition
BR-1ConcentrationConc. % = Brix × product refractive factor. GTS-120 1.29, AQ-YB 2.0. MDS-570, I-42 and G-5300ME are not refracted. In range if within low–high, allowing 0.05 for rounding.
BR-2Coolant doseBelow low limit only: add (L) = (target − reading) × 0.01 × sump L. Gallons = L × 0.264172. Above high: no product.
BR-3Comment ruleA reading still out of range after dosing, a high reading, a pH out of range or a Not Good check needs a comment before it is complete.
BR-4Bug limitsBacteria 3 or more, or fungus 2 or more, needs treatment. Same limits on every system.
BR-5Chemical dosesGrotan = sump L × 0.0015; LA-33 = sump L × 0.0002; WR-90 = sump L × 0.001. All in litres.
BR-6pH range8.9–9.6 for every system and washer. Below 8.9 is critical and prompts LA-33 (coolant) or WR-90 (washer). Above 9.6 is non-critical: note the reason.
BR-7Schedules and holidaysMWTH: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday. TF: Tuesday, Friday. Washers: weekly. Dust/mist/skimmer: every workday. Nothing is due on a plant holiday. When Monday is a holiday, the MWTH Monday check moves to Tuesday.
BR-8Mix tankMix conc. = Brix × 1.29. Expected = raw ÷ (raw + water) × 100. Flagged if more than 0.5 points from expected.
BR-9Bug slide groupsGroups A and B alternate by week; W systems are tested every week. Slides are dipped Monday and read Wednesday.
BR-10Washer chart readingInitial RI % = the chart value for the drop count. A count between two rows uses the row below (e.g. I-42, 12 drops → 11-drop row, 1.25%). Below the first row reads 0 and is flagged; above the last row shows “above chart”.
BR-11Washer doseBelow range: add (L) = (target − RI) × 0.01 × sump L, where target is mid range. I-42 0.8–1.5%, target 1.15%. G-5300ME 1.5–2.5%, target 2%.
BR-12Crew rotationGreen and Yellow crews swap days and nights every two weeks. Green moved to days on 2 November 2026.
BR-13Edit windowsTechs edit their own shift’s entries. Contractors edit only their own entries, for 5 days after saving, and can’t delete. Site Managers and Plant Admins edit and delete any entry.
BR-14Forms upload dueNext due = last upload + 7 days (Weekly) or + 30 days (Monthly). Overdue after the due date; Not uploaded if never.

9. Decisions confirmed

  • The blue side-menu layout (prototype v3) is the layout to build from.
  • G-2200TU is replaced by Inhibitor 42 (I-42) on every washer; washer dose target is mid range.
  • A drop count between chart rows uses the row below.
  • Managers edit all master data, including product names, refractive factors, ranges, targets, machine IDs, operations, schedules and titration charts.
  • Leadec’s file is uploaded as it comes; unassigned systems are kept as Leadec-only.
  • Dust collector checks come from TMMAL’s Microsoft Forms for now. Each line has its own Forms link; Sprint’s product lines differ; Tool Regrind stands alone with one dropdown choice. In-app entry stays ready for later.
  • Forms exports are uploaded weekly until the process is accepted, then possibly monthly.
  • Development starts with Sprint/TRUQ2 only.
