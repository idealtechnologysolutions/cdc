A. Business rules

#ItemPrototype assumptionConfirmCorrection
A1ConcentrationConc. % = Brix × product refractive factor. Out of range if outside low–high by more than 0.05.☐
A2Dosing amountAdd (L) = (target − reading) ÷ 100 × sump litres. Prompted only when below the low limit.☐
A3Comment ruleA reading still out of range after dosing cannot be saved without a comment.☐
A4Washer dropsFewer drops than the washer's limit = too weak, so the row is flagged.☐
A5Mix tankMix conc. = Brix × 1.29. Flagged amber if more than 0.5 points from the raw-to-water ratio.☐
A6Bug limitsBacteria 3 or more and fungus 2 or more need treatment. Same limits on every system.☐
A7pH range8.9–9.6 for every system and washer. Low pH prompts LA-33.☐
A8Chemical unitsGrotan, LA-33 and WR-90 recorded in litres. The tech enters the dose; the app doesn't suggest one.☐
A9Schedule groupsPits daily · MWTH Mon/Wed/Thu · TF Tue/Fri · Washers weekly.☐
A10Bug test groupsGroup A and B alternate by week. Weekly (W) systems are tested every week. Slides dipped Monday, read Wednesday.☐
A11HolidaysNothing is due on a holiday. A Monday holiday moves MWTH to Tuesday. No rule yet for Wednesday or Friday holidays.☐
A12SavingPick one for all screens: ☐ Save button   ☐ Save as you type—
